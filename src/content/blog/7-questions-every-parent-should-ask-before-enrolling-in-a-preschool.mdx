<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Starting preschool is a big milestone for both parents and children. It is often a child's first experience outside home, and choosing the right environment can make a huge difference in how comfortably they adapt to learning.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>With many <strong>play schools and preschools in Chennai</strong>, parents often feel confused about how to evaluate options during <strong>preschool admissions</strong>. Instead of feeling overwhelmed, asking the right questions can help you make a confident and informed decision.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Here are seven important questions every parent should ask before enrolling their child in a preschool.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>1. What Teaching Method Does the Preschool Follow?</strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Not all preschools teach the same way. Some follow play-based learning, some use Montessori methods, while others combine multiple approaches.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The best preschools focus on learning through activities like storytelling, games, music, and creative play rather than pushing academics too early. A good learning method helps children enjoy school while building curiosity and confidence.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>2. How Many Children Are in Each Class?</strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Class size matters a lot during early childhood. Young children need personal attention to feel comfortable and secure in a new environment.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Preschools with smaller teacher-student ratios allow teachers to understand each child's needs better, provide emotional support, and help children adjust smoothly.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>3. Is the School Safe and Child-Friendly?</strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Safety is one of the biggest concerns for parents during <strong>preschool admissions</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>When visiting a school, observe whether the environment feels clean, secure, and welcoming. Look for basic safety measures like monitored entry points, trained staff, and safe play areas. A secure environment helps children explore freely and builds parent confidence.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>4. Does the Preschool Focus on Overall Development?</strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>A good preschool is not just about alphabets and numbers. It should help children grow socially, emotionally, and physically.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Activities such as art, music, storytelling, and group play help children develop communication skills, creativity, and confidence. A preschool that supports <strong>holistic development and 360-degree learning</strong> prepares children naturally for kindergarten.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>5. Are the Teachers Caring and Experienced?</strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Teachers play a very important role during preschool years. Children need patient, warm, and supportive educators who understand early childhood behaviour.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Parents should observe how teachers interact with children. Friendly communication and a nurturing attitude help children feel safe and happy at school.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>6. How Does the School Communicate With Parents?</strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Regular communication between parents and the school is essential. It helps parents understand their child's progress and stay involved in their learning journey.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Good preschools provide updates through meetings, activity reports, and regular feedback, ensuring parents always know how their child is adapting.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>7. What Are the Fees and Admission Process?</strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Before finalising admission, parents should clearly understand the full fee structure. Apart from tuition, schools may charge for activities, materials, or transport.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Preschool fees in Chennai vary depending on facilities and curriculum, so it is important to compare options and choose a school that fits both your expectations and budget.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Why These Questions Matter</strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Choosing a preschool is not just about convenience or reputation. It is about finding a place where your child feels safe, happy, and encouraged to learn.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>By asking these simple but important questions, parents can select a preschool that supports their child's emotional comfort, learning readiness, and overall development.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
