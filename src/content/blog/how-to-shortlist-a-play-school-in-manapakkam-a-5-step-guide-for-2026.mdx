<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Admission season is here, and if you live in Manapakkam, Porur, or Ramapuram, you are probably visiting schools, comparing options, and trying to figure out which play school is actually the right fit for your child.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>There are good schools in this area. But "good" means different things to different families. What matters is finding the right match – for your child's age, your daily routine, and your family's expectations.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This 5-step guide helps you do exactly that, without wasting time or getting overwhelmed.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Step 1 – Start with Location and Timing, Not Reputation</strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The most common mistake Chennai parents make is chasing a school's name before checking something more basic: can your child actually get there on time, every day, without stress?</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>A play school that is 6 km away in Manapakkam peak traffic is not the same as one that is 1.5 km away. For a toddler aged 1.5 to 3 years, a long commute means arriving tired and unsettled – before the school day has even started.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Start your shortlist with schools within 2 km of your home or your drop-off route. In Manapakkam, this naturally covers areas like Porur, Ramapuram, Alapakkam, and Mugalivakkam. Once you have a geography-based shortlist, then look at reputation, curriculum, and fees.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Step 2 – Match the Programme to Your Child's Age</strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Not every play school in Manapakkam offers every programme. And not every programme is right for every age. Before calling a school, know exactly which programme your child needs.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Playgroup</strong> is for children aged 1.5 to 2.5 years. Sessions are shorter, the pace is gentle, and the focus is entirely on play and sensory exploration. This is the right starting point if your child is under 2.5 years.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Nursery</strong> is for children aged 2.5 to 3.5 years. More structured, with language activities, group play, and early social skills built into the day.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>LKG</strong> is for children aged 3.5 to 4.5 years. This is where foundational learning – phonics, numbers, creative expression – begins in a more formal sense.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>When you call a school, ask directly: "Does my child's birth date qualify for this programme?" Age cutoffs vary between schools, and confirming this first saves you from visiting schools your child cannot even join.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Step 3 – Visit in Person and Watch, Not Just Listen</strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Every play school in Manapakkam will tell you they have trained teachers, a safe environment, and a great curriculum. That is what every school says. What tells you the truth is what you see when you visit.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>When you walk in, ignore the brochure and the principal's office. Go straight to the classroom – or ask to observe one. Watch how the teachers interact with the children. Are they warm and patient? Do they get down to the child's level? Does the classroom feel busy and happy, or rushed and noisy?</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Also check the basics that parents often forget to look at: Is the bathroom clean and child-sized? Is the outdoor play area safe and shaded – important in Chennai's heat? Is the entrance gate secure?</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Come with specific questions ready:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>What is the teacher-to-student ratio in Playgroup and Nursery?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>How do you handle a child who is upset at drop-off?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Do you send daily updates to parents?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Is there a Daycare option after school hours?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>A school that answers these openly and specifically is a school that has nothing to hide.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Step 4 – Understand the Full Fee Structure Before You Decide</strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Preschool fees in Manapakkam vary widely – from ₹25,000 to ₹70,000 per year depending on the school, programme, and facilities. But the number on the brochure is rarely the full picture.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Many schools in Manapakkam and across Chennai charge separately for admission fees, activity materials, annual day events, uniform, and transport. A school that looks affordable at first glance can become significantly more expensive once all of these are added.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>When you visit, ask for a complete written fee breakdown – not just the annual programme fee. Ask specifically:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Is the admission fee refundable if we change our mind?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>What is included in the fees and what is charged separately?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>What is the Daycare fee if we need extended hours?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Knowing the true cost upfront avoids surprises and helps you compare schools on an equal basis.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Step 5 – Trust What Your Child Does During the Demo Class</strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Most good play schools in Manapakkam offer a demo class or trial visit before admission. This is not just a formality – it is the most useful data point you have.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Bring your child. Let them spend 30₹45 minutes in the classroom with the teacher and other children. Then watch. Not for whether they cry – many children do initially, and that is completely normal. Watch for how the teacher responds. Does she get down to your child's level? Does she gently include them in the activity? Does she make your child feel seen?</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After the visit, notice what your child says or does at home. Do they talk about the toys? Do they ask to go back? Even small signs of curiosity or interest are meaningful signals from a child who cannot yet fully articulate what they feel.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Your instinct as a parent also matters. If the classroom felt warm and purposeful when you walked in, trust that. If something felt off – even if you cannot name it – trust that too.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Find the Right Start for Your Child</strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Shortlisting a play school does not need to be stressful. With a clear process, two or three school visits, and the right questions ready, most parents find the right fit within a week.</p>
