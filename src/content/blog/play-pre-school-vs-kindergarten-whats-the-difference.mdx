<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Introduction</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>When parents begin exploring early education options, they often come across two terms that sound similar – Play School (or Pre School) and Kindergarten. While both play important roles in a child's early learning journey, they differ in age group, curriculum style, teaching approach, and developmental goals.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Understanding these differences helps parents choose the right starting point and support their child through each stage of development.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">What Is a Play School or Pre School?</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>A Play School / Pre School is a child's first introduction to structured learning. Typically designed for children aged 2 to 4 years, it focuses on play-based, sensory, and experiential learning.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Key Characteristics of Play / Pre School</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Focus on sensory, motor, and emotional development<br></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Learning through play, music, movement, stories, and hands-on activities<br></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Shorter school hours and a flexible schedule<br></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Builds confidence, curiosity, and social skills<br></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Uses methodologies like Playway and Montessori</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Objective: Create a nurturing environment where children explore, express, and adapt comfortably to group settings.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">What Is Kindergarten?</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Kindergarten is the next step after Pre School, typically for children aged 4 to 6 years. It serves as the bridge between early learning and formal schooling, introducing structured concepts in age-appropriate ways.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Key Characteristics of Kindergarten</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Emphasis on early literacy (phonics, reading) and numeracy<br></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Introduction to structured subjects like language, math, and general awareness<br></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Development of writing and comprehension<br></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Activities include problem-solving, phonics, science exploration, and pre-academics<br></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Prepares children for Grade 1</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Objective: Build academic readiness while supporting emotional, cognitive, and social development.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">How These Two Stages Work Together</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Play School and Kindergarten are not alternatives – they are complementary learning stages.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Pre School helps children adapt socially, emotionally, and behaviorally.<br></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Kindergarten builds on this foundation to introduce structured academics.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Together, they support holistic and 360-degree development, preparing children for lifelong learning.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Which Is Right for Your Child?</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Choose a Play School if your child:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->

<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Needs support in social and emotional development,<br></li>

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Is taking their first step into group learning.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Choose Kindergarten if your child:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Is between 4₹6 years,<br></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Is ready for structured learning,<br></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Needs preparation for Grade 1 and formal academics.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The right environment ensures your child grows confidently at every stage.</p>
