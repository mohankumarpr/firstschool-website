<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Nanganallur is one of Chennai's most family-friendly neighbourhoods, making it a preferred choice for parents looking for quality play schools and preschools. With several options available, choosing the right school for your child can feel overwhelming – especially during admission season.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This admissions checklist is designed to help parents evaluate play schools in Nanganallur step by step, ensuring the school you choose supports your child's early childhood development, learning readiness, and emotional growth.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Step 1: Check the Right Age Criteria</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Before shortlisting a play school, confirm that your child meets the age eligibility:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Play Group: 2 – 3 years</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Pre School / Nursery: 3 – 4 years</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Kindergarten (LKG &amp; UKG): 4 – 6 years</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Most preschools in Nanganallur strictly follow age guidelines aligned with early education standards.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Step 2: Understand the Curriculum &amp; Teaching Approach</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Different schools follow different early learning methods. Look for a curriculum that matches your child's learning style.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Common options include:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Play-based learning – learning through activities, music, and movement</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Montessori method – hands-on, self-paced learning</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Integrated curriculum – a blend of play and structured academics</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The best play schools in Nanganallur focus on holistic development and 360-degree learning, not rote academics.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Step 3: Evaluate Teacher Quality &amp; Class Size</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Teachers play a vital role in early childhood education. During your visit, check:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Teacher qualifications and early childhood training</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Teacher-student ratio (smaller groups allow better attention)</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>How teachers interact with children during class</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>A nurturing and patient teaching approach helps children feel safe and confident.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Step 4: Review Infrastructure &amp; Safety Measures</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Safety is non-negotiable for parents choosing a play school.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Ensure the school has:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Clean, child-friendly classrooms</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Secure entry and exit points</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>CCTV monitoring</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Trained support staff</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Safe play areas with age-appropriate equipment</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Good infrastructure supports both learning and well-being.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Step 5: Focus on Learning Beyond Academics</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>A strong preschool programme supports more than academics.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Look for schools that encourage:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Social skills and peer interaction</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Emotional development</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Language and communication skills</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Creativity through art, music, and storytelling</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This balanced approach prepares children for kindergarten and formal schooling.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Step 6: Ask About Parent Engagement &amp; Communication</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Top play schools in Nanganallur maintain regular communication with parents.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Check if the school offers:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Parent-teacher meetings</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Progress updates</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Events and open-house sessions</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Transparent feedback systems</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Parental involvement strengthens a child's learning journey.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Passport-size photographs</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Parent ID proof</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Completed application form</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Some schools may conduct informal interaction sessions rather than tests.</p>
